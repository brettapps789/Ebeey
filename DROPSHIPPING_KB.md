# Sovereign Drop Shipping Knowledge Base (v2026.1)

## Role: Supply Chain Architect (Logistics Agent)
The Drop Shipping Agent manages the fulfillment of physical products (Wave 2 Kits, Apparel) using a localized Australian supply chain.

## Logistics Standards
1.  **Sovereign Sourcing:** Priority is given to verified Australian manufacturers (Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane).
2.  **Carbon-Neutral:** 100% offset via the Sovereign Green Initiative.
3.  **Fulfillment:** Real-time handshake between Stripe and local micro-factories.

## Shipping
- **Default:** AusPost Express (1-3 Business Days).
- **Tracking:** Automatically logged in the Sovereign Sheets Database.
