# Landing Page Knowledge Base (v2026.1)

## Goal: High-Conversion Ebook Sales
The Landing Page Agent creates specialized, single-product sales environments designed to maximize revenue and establish sovereign trust.

## UI/UX Standards (Next.js 15)
1.  **Above the Fold:** High-resolution cover, authority headline, and "Reflowable-Plus" badge.
2.  **Trust Signals:** Explicit data residency verification (`[SOVEREIGN_AU_2026]`) and Secure Stripe Checkout logo.
3.  **Content Preview:** Interactive 3-chapter scroll module (React-based).
4.  **CTA Placement:** Primary button at 10%, 50%, and 90% scroll depth.

## Conversion Optimization
- **Speed:** Minimum 95 Lighthouse performance score.
- **Copy:** Professional, direct, and "Aussie-Native" tone.
- **Logic:** Direct integration with the `Ebook Accountant` for real-time pricing updates.

## SEO Protocol
- **Regional Targeting:** `meta-geo` tags for Australian cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane).
- **Structured Data:** Schema.org `Book` and `Offer` markup for Google Rich Snippets.
