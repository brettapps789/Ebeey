# Chapter 4: The Horizons Storefront: Scaling with AI

## Building Your Ebook Empire in Minutes

With the architecture defined and the content generated, the final hurdle is distribution. In the Ebeey ecosystem, this is handled by **Hostinger Horizons**. By leveraging this AI-powered app builder, we can transform a collection of Markdown files into a high-converting, global storefront with zero manual coding.

The Horizons Integration Strategy:
1.  **AI-Generated UI:** Describe your brand aesthetic to the Horizons chatbot, and it will construct a React-based frontend optimized for book sales.
2.  **Serverless Scaling:** Your storefront is automatically deployed on Hostinger's global infrastructure, ensuring fast load times for readers in every corner of the world.
3.  **Unified API Management:** Using the `HORIZONS_API_KEY`, our pipeline can programmatically update the storefront as new chapters of "Ebeey" are released.

In this chapter, we walk through the setup of your first Horizons app and how to connect it to the Ebeey metadata service for real-time synchronization.
