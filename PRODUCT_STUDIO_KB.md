# Sovereign Product Studio Knowledge Base (v2026.1)

## Role: Industrial Design Architect (Product Agent)
The Product Design Agent architects both physical and digital products, ensuring they meet the high-fidelity standards of the Sovereign Aussie AI Workforce.

## Design Standards (2026)
1.  **Aesthetic:** "Outback Cyberpunk" - Brushed metals, holographic interfaces, and earthy tones.
2.  **Materials:** 
    *   **Tier 1:** Titanium and Graphene (High-End).
    *   **Tier 2:** Recycled Ocean Plastics (Sustainable).
3.  **Jurisdiction:** 100% Australian manufacturing and hosting verification.

## Product Types
- **Physical:** Hardware kits (Aussie Nodes), Apparel (Sovereign Techwear), and Accessories.
- **Digital:** Interactive modules, NFT-based intellectual property, and custom API access.

## Creation Workflow
1.  **Ideation:** Architect provides a concept prompt.
2.  **Spec Generation:** Trigger `generate_product_blueprint`.
3.  **Financial Audit:** Accountant Agent verifies unit cost and net margin.
4.  **Mockup:** OpenAI/Vertex AI generates visual assets.
5.  **Listing:** Storefront Builder Agent pushes to the live `/shop`.

## Ethical Compliance
- All physical products must include the `[SOVEREIGN_AU_2026]` origin mark.
- Digital products must verify data residency in `australia-southeast1`.
